- **fix(rate-limit):** a request that gives up on the local rate-limit queue (queue-wait budget or client abort) now leaves the Bottleneck queue instead of staying there as dead work. Before, the abandoned job was still dispatched later and spent a reservoir token and a `minTime` slot before rejecting, so with a per-connection `rateLimitOverrides.rpm` live requests queued behind dead ones, timed out, and added more dead jobs: the connection's queue never recovered while traffic continued. Jobs already past the queue (waiting out `minTime`) are unchanged.
