- **fix(translator):** the OpenAI → Gemini request translator no longer injects a default `thinkingBudget` that is larger than the client's `max_tokens` / `max_completion_tokens`. Gemini counts thinking against `maxOutputTokens`, so a request with `max_tokens: 50` and no reasoning knob went upstream with `thinkingBudget: 24576` and the model could spend the whole cap thinking, returning an empty `content`. The injected (not client-requested) budget is now capped at half of the output cap, but not below the minimum Google documents for the 2.5 Pro (128) and 2.5 Flash-Lite (512) tiers; explicit `reasoning_effort` and `thinking.budget_tokens` budgets are unchanged.
