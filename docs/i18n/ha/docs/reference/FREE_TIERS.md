## TL;DR — adadin inferens na kyauta nawa ne OmniRoute ke tarawa a zahiri?

Waɗannan alƙaluma suna bayyana asusun ɓangarori na uku daban-daban da sharuɗɗan cancanta a faɗin kundin. OmniRoute ba ya ba ku wannan kasafi: dole ne ku samo asusu ko bayanan shaidar da ake buƙata, ku haɗa masu samarwa da suka cancanta, kuma ku kiyaye iyakokinsu. Ainihin ƙarfin da za a iya amfani da shi na iya zama ƙasa, har ma ya zama sifili a sabon shigarwa wanda ba shi da hanyar da ta cancanta. Masu samarwa da aka yi wa alamar `tos: avoid` ba sa shiga cikin zaɓin hanya ta atomatik a tsari na asali.

**An bincika 2026-10-08:** [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/pricing) yana bayar da kuɗin talla na $5 sau ɗaya wanda ke buƙatar hanyar biyan kuɗi, kuma wa'adinsa zai ƙare bayan kwanaki 30. An sabunta dukan kundin bayan gudanar da binciken yanar gizo ta wakilai 50 (takardun hukuma + labaran kwanaki 7 da suka gabata, tare da tantancewa ta hanyar ƙalubale). Muhimman bayanai:

- **An cire / babu matakin kyauta (2026):** `chutes` (matakin kyauta ya ƙare 2026-03), `phind` (kamfanin ya rufe 2026-01), `kluster` (an dakatar da shi 2026-06-09 → MITO), `gitlawb` + `gitlawb-gmi` (an janye kyautar MiMo 2026-05-24, tallan Nemotron ya ƙare 2026-06 — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `aimlapi` (an dakatar da matakin kyauta — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `yi` (an daina Yi-Light, ya koma biyan kuɗi gwargwadon amfani — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `featherless-ai` (babu matakin kyauta a halin yanzu), `chipotle` ("Pepper AI" na Chipotle — widget ɗin taɗi na Amelia da aka gano aikinsa ta hanyar nazarin baya a `amelia.chipotle.com`, wanda mai samarwar ke sadarwa da shi, yanzu yana mayar da 404 a kowace hanya, har da tushen; an daina ko an matsar da backend ɗin, an tabbatar 2026-09-15 — an cire shi gaba ɗaya daga kundin, #13131/#4037). `iflytek` / `sparkdesk` sun ci gaba da kasancewa cikin jeri amma suna ɗauke da bayanin taka-tsantsan game da ToS (Spark Lite kyauta ne; ToS ɗin yana ƙuntata amfani da proxy/relay).
- **Gemini** — an rufe `2.0 Flash` / `2.0 Flash-Lite` a 2026-06-01 kuma `2.5 Pro` ya fita daga matakin kyauta (2026-04); yanzu matakin kyauta na **iyalan Flash kawai** ne (2.5/3/3.1/3.5 Flash + Gemma). Yanzu kundin yana **haɗa** iyalan Flash a tafki guda (a baya an kumbura adadin ta hanyar ƙirga kowace siga dabam: 462M → 60M).
- **Adadin da aka gyara:** `cloudflare-ai` 122M → **30M** (ainihin 10k-Neurons/rana), an sake rarraba `doubao` a matsayin kiredit na rajista na lokaci guda (ba mai maimaituwa ba), `llm7` 4M → **150M** (an rubuta token 5M/rana), an dakatar da endpoints na `together` masu "-Free" → an kuma cire kiredit na rajista na $25 (ana buƙatar mafi ƙarancin saye na $5, babu gwaji kyauta), lokacin Preview na `longcat` ya ƙare + an daina samfuran Flash → **LongCat-2.0** kawai, an sake rarraba shi a matsayin kiredit na rajista na lokaci guda na token **10M** (yana buƙatar KYC, ba mai maimaituwa ba).

## Tsaruka biyu — ƙirgawa da yanke hukunci

OmniRoute yana amsa "shin kyauta ne?" ta hanyar tsaruka biyu waɗanda aka tsara da gangan su karanta daga tushe daban-daban:

| Tsari                | Tushen gaskiya                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Wuraren bayyanawa                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ƙirgawa / nunawa** | Kasidar da aka tantance — tushen da aka aika tare da sakin manhaja wanda bayanan Radar suka ɗora a kai (`getRadarCatalog`)                                                                                      | Jimillar matakin kyauta, katin kasafin kuɗi, allunan bayanai                                                                    |
| **Yanke hukunci**    | Kasidar da aka aika tare da sakin manhaja kawai (`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS` a cikin `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.data.ts`) tare da ƙa'idodin hasashen gida (`:free` suffix, farashin sifili, `grantsFreeAccess`) | Duk masu amfani da `src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`: shigo da samfurin, turawar `auto/*`, `GET /v1/models`, da samfotin burauza |

Ƙirgawa na iya inganta duk lokacin da ake samun tushen bayanai. Yanke hukunci yana ci gaba da dogaro da kayan sakin manhaja, don haka amsar tana zama iri ɗaya a burauza da sabar, ana iya maimaita ta ba tare da intanet ba, kuma ana iya gwada ta ba tare da rumbun bayanai ba. Barin samfotin burauza ya karanta tushe ɗaya yayin da shigo da bayanan sabar ke karanta wani zai haifar da samfotin da bai dace da abin da ke faruwa bayan dannawa ba — ana kiyaye wannan rabuwar da gangan.

## Hanya & abubuwan lura

- Lambobin **kimantawa ne na iyaka mafi girma** daga iyakokin matakin kyauta da kowane mai samarwa ya rubuta zuwa ranar **2026-06-17**, waɗanda aka tattara ta hanyar binciken yanar gizo. Matakan kyauta suna sauyawa koyaushe — sake tabbatarwa kafin dogaro da kowace lamba.
- **Abin da kowace shigarwa take tabbatarwa a zahiri.** Babu wata shigarwa da ke ɗauke da ƙimar amincewa ta kowane layi, kuma API ba ya bayar da wata — ɗauki kowace lambar da ke sama a matsayin kimantawa mai inganci iri ɗaya wanda ba a bayyana ba. Abubuwa biyu sun bambanta, saboda an tsara su da hannu maimakon a yi hasashensu: shigarwa 44 suna ɗauke da iyakar tsayawa tabbatacciya da aka rubuta a wani tushe mai zaman kansa (39 daga cikinsu layukan xKiro ne, waɗanda duk suke amfani da kaso ɗaya na kullum), kuma shigarwa 13 suna ɗauke da bayanin amfani da umarni wajen horarwa. Ana saita `hardStopGuaranteed` ne kawai idan sharuɗɗan mai samarwar da kansa suka ce wuce adadin kyauta zai sa a ƙi buƙatar maimakon fara cajin ku ba tare da sanarwa ba, tare da tushe a cikin tsokaci kusa da shigarwar; ba a taɓa saita shi zuwa `true` ta tsohuwa ba, kuma shigarwar da babu wanda ya tabbatar da ita tana kasancewa ba a saita ba. Saboda haka, rashin alamar tsayawa tabbatacciya yana nufin "ba a tabbatar ba", ba "an san zai caje ku" ba. Yanayin **STRICT** (kariyar turawa ta zaɓi `freeAccessPolicy=strict`) yana amincewa ne kawai da shigarwar da ke ɗauke da alamar; ana cire duk sauran matakan kyauta a matsayin `no-hard-stop` (duba `open-sse/services/autoCombo/strictZeroCostFilter.ts`). Lokacin da kariyar ta tace rukuni, kundin rajistar da mai gudanarwa ke gani yana bayar da rahoton adadin waɗanda aka cire da kuma rabon `no-hard-stop` nasu.
- `estMonthlyFreeTokens` = token na wata-wata masu maimaituwa kawai. **Kiredit na rajista na lokaci ɗaya ba ya maimaituwa** kuma ana ƙirga shi a matsayin 0. Matakan da aka dakatar su ma 0 ne.
- Iyakar token ta kullum → `monthly = daily × 30`. Idan RPD kawai aka rubuta → `RPD × ~800 output tokens × 30`. Idan RPM/TPM kawai ne (babu iyakar kullum) → **ba shi da iyaka** (duba ƙasa).
- **Kyauta ta dindindin, amma babu iyakar token da aka wallafa** (`siliconflow`, `glm-cn`, `tencent`, `baidu`, `kilo-gateway`, `opencode-zen`, `gemini`, `ollama-cloud`): waɗannan dama ce ta kyauta mai maimaituwa ta gaske, wadda aka iyakance ta fuskar yawan buƙatu/aiki lokaci guda. Muna rarraba su a matsayin `recurring-uncapped` kuma **ba ma taɓa haɗa jimillarsu** — ninka `RPM × 24/7 × 30d` zai samar da iyakar ƙage (kumburin da muka ƙi). An jera su domin ku san suna wanzuwa.
- **Ƙarin da ajiya ke buɗewa:** ƙaramin ƙarin kuɗi na lokaci ɗaya wanda ke ɗaga adadin kyauta har abada (OpenRouter: $10 → 1000 req/day ≈ +24M/mo). Ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin wata lamba dabam, kuma ba a haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin.
- **Adadin da ya dogara da cancanta** (`eligibilityGate: "regional-identity"`): adadin maimaituwa na gaske wanda yake buɗewa ne kawai bayan tantance shaida mai alaƙa da yanki (tantance ainihin suna a babban yankin China a yau). Ana ƙirga shi ta amfani da ƙa'idar cire maimaituwar rukuni iri ɗaya zuwa wata lamba dabam (`gatedRecurringTokens`), kuma ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin. Tsarin (`freeType`) bai canza ba, don haka turawa ma ba ta canza ba.
- **Rukunin hujjoji.** Ƙa'idar ita ce: lamba a cikin kasidar tana ambaton tushenta a cikin tsokacin `// evidence:` kusa da shigarwar — `public-page` (shafin mai samarwa da kowa zai iya karantawa), `api-public` (endpoint na mai samarwa wanda ba ya buƙatar tantancewa, misali endpoint na tsare-tsaren jama'a na NaraRouter a router.bynara.id), ko `console-verified <date> por <who>` (ana iya ganin lambar ne kawai a cikin console na asusu; tsokacin yana rubuta wanda ya gan ta da lokacin da ya gani, da kuma shafin jama'a da ke cewa iyakar tana wanzuwa). Matsayin yanzu: rukuni biyar da aka sake bincikawa a ranar 2026-09-02 suna ɗauke da shi (`gemini`, `groq`, `mistral`, `ollama-cloud`, `nara`); shigarwar da suka gabaci sake binciken 2026-09-02 suna gaji binciken da ya gabata har sai an taɓa su; duk wata lamba **sabuwa ko da aka canza** wadda ba ta da tsokacin hujja kuskure ce. A yau `mistral` kawai aka tabbatar ta hanyar console.

## Me ya sa adadinmu ya fi na sauran masu tattarawa ƙanƙanta

Yawancin alkaluman "free tokens per month" a wannan fanni jimillar lambobin da aka sanya wa kowane samfurin AI ne. Namu ba haka yake ba, da gangan:

- **Ana ƙirga kowane asusun bai ɗaya sau ɗaya kawai.** Tsarin kyauta na Mistral yana da izinin 1B/month guda ɗaya ga kowace ƙungiya; jera shi a ƙarƙashin samfura biyar ba ya mai da shi 5B. Idan aka tara bisa kowane samfurin AI, kundin namu zai nuna **~7.4B** (an sake lissafawa a 2026-09-03; wannan adadin ba ya ƙarƙashin kulawar CI — a sake auna shi duk lokacin da kundin ya canza) — babban taken yana cewa **~1.47B** domin wannan ne ainihin abin da asusu ɗaya na kowane mai samarwa zai iya kashewa.
- **Ana sauya iyakokin yau da kullum, amma ba ƙididdigar saurin amfani ba.** Iyakacin tokens/day da aka rubuta yana zama `× 30`; iyakacin requests/day da aka rubuta yana zama `RPD × ~800 tokens × 30`; mai samarwa wanda kawai yake wallafa requests-per-minute **ba shi da** adadin wata-wata kuma ana jera shi a matsayin _uncapped_, ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin jimilla ba. Ninka iyakar sauri da 24/7 shi ne kumburin ƙididdiga da muka ƙi yi.
- **Ana nuna ƙayyadaddun kaso da ke buƙatar tantance shaidar yanki daban** (`+~6M behind regional identity verification`), domin yawancin masu karatu ba za su iya amfani da su ba.
- **Kiredit na rajista na watan farko ne kawai** kuma ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin adadi na biyu, ba a taɓa haɗa shi da adadin dindindin ba.
- **CI ne ke tabbatar da adadin.** `npm run check:docs-counts` yana sake lissafa jimilloli daga kundin kuma yana hana ginin kammalawa idan wannan fayil, README ko katin kasafin kuɗi sun kauce daga gare su.

## Teburin bayanan kula na ToS

> **Alamar ToS shawara ce, ba ƙofar sarrafa zirga-zirga ba.** Masu samarwa da aka yi wa alamar `tos` har yanzu ana haɗa su cikin sarrafa zirga-zirga da combo/fallback ta tsoho; alamar tana bayyana ne kawai a `/dashboard/free-tiers` da `/api/free-tier/summary`. Hukuncin yana cikin `open-sse/config/freeTierCatalog.ts` (na bayani ne, injunan sarrafa zirga-zirga ba sa karanta shi).

> Taƙaitaccen nazari kan sharuɗɗan kowane mai samarwa game da wakili na sirri da mutum ɗaya ke amfani da shi kuma ya karɓi bakuncinsa da kansa. `caution` = akwai sharadin amfani na sirri ko na wakili da ya kamata a bincika; `ambiguous` = ba a fayyace ba; `ok` = an ba da izini a sarari. Wannan bayani ne kawai, ba shawarar doka ba — kai ne za ka yanke hukunci.
